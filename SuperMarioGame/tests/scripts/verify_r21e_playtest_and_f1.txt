# R21 Lane E - Playtest (F5) out of the editor and back, then the in-game F1
# editor that PlayingState still hosts.
#
# The F5 route is the "how do I play it" answer that needs no menu trip: the
# level is round-tripped through a scratch file (so the playtest proves the file
# will load, not just the in-memory state) and the run is pushed OVER the
# editor, so quitting it pops back to the level still being authored rather than
# stranding it under a main menu.
#
#   ./build_r21_lane_e/SuperMarioGame --script tests/scripts/verify_r21e_playtest_and_f1.txt

wait 1.5

# MAP EDITOR, then open World 1-1 so the playtest has a real level to run.
press Down
press Down
press Down
press Down
wait 0.3
press Enter
wait 1.5
press O ctrl
wait 0.8
press Enter
wait 1.5

# F5: playtest.
press F5
wait 2.5
shot r21e_20_playtest_running
hold D 1.2
wait 2.0
shot r21e_21_playtest_moving

# Esc opens the pause overlay; QUIT TO MENU is its fifth row and, for a
# playtest, pops back to the editor.
press Escape
wait 0.8
press Down
press Down
press Down
press Down
wait 0.3
press Enter
wait 1.5
shot r21e_22_back_in_editor

# And the in-game F1 editor over a normal run, which must still work.
press Escape
wait 1.2
press Enter
wait 1.0
press Enter
wait 1.5
press Enter
wait 3.0
press F1
wait 1.0
shot r21e_23_f1_ingame_editor
quit
